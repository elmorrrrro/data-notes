## 1. Always a beat, mostly a fall

<BeatReactions />

Since May 2024, Nvidia has beaten the analysts' revenue and profit forecasts every single quarter, by
**{s.avg_rev_beat}%** of revenue on average. And yet the stock fell the next day **{s.falls} times out of {s.n}**.
That is not as strange as it sounds. A forecast is what analysts write down. What moves the price is what investors
already paid for. When everyone knows Nvidia will beat, a beat is the starting point, not the news.

So the real question is not whether Nvidia beats on November 17. It almost certainly will. The question is by how much,
and whether that is more than investors already expect.

## 2. Revenue: the size of the beat mattered a little

<BeatScatter metric="revenue" />

Put each report on a chart, with the size of the revenue beat across and the next day's move up and down, and a rough
line shows up at about **{s.cut}%**. Of the four reports at or above it, three came with a rise. Of the six below it,
only one did: May 2025, when Nvidia had just lost about $8 billion of China sales to export rules, investors expected
worse, and the stock had already gained 24% in the month before.

Two of the three big-beat rises also had something extra. In May 2024, Nvidia announced a 10-for-1 stock split together
with the results. In August 2026, Jensen Huang said revenue could grow about 70% next fiscal year, while analysts had
pencilled in 44%. A big beat helped, but it was rarely the only news.

## 3. The next-quarter forecast: no pattern at all

<BeatScatter metric="guidance" />

With every report, Nvidia also says what it expects to sell the next quarter. You would think a big jump there matters
most. In these ten reports, it did not. The biggest forecast beat, **+{s.max_guide_beat.guidance_beat_pct.toFixed(1)}%**
in February 2026, was followed by a **{s.max_guide_beat.move_pct.toFixed(1)}%** day. The only time the forecast came in
*below* what analysts wanted, in May 2025, the stock rose.

For November 17, Wall Street expects Nvidia to forecast about **${s.next.street_next_q} billion** for the quarter after.
Going by the last ten reports, beating that number is no guarantee of a good day.

## 5. The part that has never happened before

The size of the beat was not the strongest pattern in the data. How the stock ran into the report was. In the four
reports where Nvidia's stock had risen most in the 20 trading days before, it rose three times after. In the four
where it had risen least, it fell every time. In all ten, though, the stock went into the report **up**: the weakest
run-in was **+{s.min_run_in}%**.

This time it goes in **{s.next.run_in_pct}%** over the last 20 trading days. That is outside everything in our data,
and it can be read both ways:

- **Bulls:** after a fall like that, expectations are low. A normal beat could be enough this time.
- **Bears:** in the past, the weaker the run-in, the worse the next day. A falling stock has kept falling.

The ten reports cannot settle that. November 17 will.

## 6. The duel so far: 2 : 2

Our video put the case for and against Nvidia in four rounds before the report:

- **Sales, point for the bulls.** Revenue last quarter: $96.2 billion, more than double the $46.7 billion of a year
  earlier.
- **Price, point for the bulls.** About $11 for every dollar of profit analysts expect next year. Microsoft: about 22.
  Amazon: 24. Apple: 33.
- **Customers, point for the bears.** In the first half of this fiscal year, just three customers brought in 16%, 15%
  and 13% of Nvidia's revenue: 44% together. If one of them slows down, Nvidia feels it.
- **The boss, point for the bears.** Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang, and House
  committee chairs asked for a closed-door briefing. The board says it has "no reason to believe that either national
  security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised", and Huang remains CEO.

## What the numbers leave out

- **Ten reports is a tiny sample.** The {s.cut}% line is where rises and falls happened to split in these ten reports,
  not a law. One more report on either side of it changes the score.
- **One day is short.** We measure from the close before the report to the next day's close. A week later, the picture
  can look different.
- **Other news on the same day.** A stock split (May 2024), China export rules (May 2025) and Huang's long-range forecast
  (August 2026) arrived with the results. We cannot separate them from the beat.
- **Consensus differs by source.** LSEG, FactSet, Yahoo Finance and others poll different analysts. Where CNBC did not
  give an LSEG number, we used the closest one (see Methodology). The difference is usually a few hundred million
  dollars, small next to the beats.
- **Profit and margins.** Nvidia's profit per share also beat every time (Yahoo Finance data), so it cannot explain the
  difference between rises and falls. We did not test gross margin, which some investors watch closely.

## Methodology

- **Revenue and consensus:** Nvidia's revenue for each quarter, its forecast for the next quarter and the LSEG consensus
  for both, as quoted by CNBC on the day of each report:
  [Aug 2024](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/08/28/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2025.html) (revenue; the forecast consensus
  of $31.77 billion is LSEG as quoted by [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/nvidia-shares-sink-amidst-tepid-guidance-beat-blackwell-mask-change/)),
  [Nov 2024](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/11/20/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q3-2025.html),
  [Feb 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/02/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q4-2025.html),
  [May 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/28/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q1-2026.html),
  [Aug 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/27/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2026.html),
  [Nov 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/19/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q3-2026.html),
  [Feb 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/investingclub/2026/02/25/nvidia-keeps-the-ai-party-alive-with-a-booming-quarter-and-even-better-outlook.html),
  [May 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/investingclub/2026/05/20/were-raising-our-price-target-on-nvidia-after-another-knockout-quarter-and-guide-.html),
  [Aug 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/investingclub/2026/08/26/nvidia-wows-wall-street-with-a-strong-quarter-eye-popping-sales-forecast.html).
  May 2024: Reuters, via [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/nvidia-forecasts-quarterly-revenue-above-estimates-unveils-stock-split-124052301333_1.html)
  (LSEG). Where Nvidia gave a range ("plus or minus 2%"), we use the middle.
- **Beat:** revenue ÷ consensus − 1. Forecast beat: Nvidia's forecast for the next quarter ÷ the consensus for that
  quarter − 1.
- **Stock move:** close on the report day (Nvidia reports after the US market closes) to the next trading day's close,
  from Yahoo Finance via `yfinance` (unadjusted closes; the 2024 stock split does not affect a one-day change).
  Run-in: change over the 20 trading days before the report day's close. Correlations with the next-day move across
  the ten reports: revenue beat {s.corr.revenue_beat_pct}, forecast beat {s.corr.guidance_beat_pct}, run-in
  {s.corr.run_in_pct}.
- **The {s.cut}% line:** the revenue beat that best splits the ten reports into rises and falls. ${s.next.need} billion is
  ${s.next.street} billion × (1 + {s.cut}%).
- **November 17 consensus:** average of {s.next.analysts} analysts on Yahoo Finance: ${s.next.street} billion for the
  quarter Nvidia reports, ${s.next.street_next_q} billion for the next one. Nvidia's own forecast of $108 billion, plus or
  minus 2%, is from its August 26, 2026 results.
- **Profit per share:** estimate and reported figure for each report from the Yahoo Finance earnings calendar.
- **The four rounds:** revenue of $96.2 billion vs $46.7 billion a year earlier from CNBC (links above). Price per dollar
  of next year's profit: forward P/E from Yahoo Finance on November 9, 2026. Customer concentration: Nvidia's
  [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/) for the quarter ended July 26, 2026,
  "Concentration of Revenue". Investigation and briefing request:
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/08/nvidia-jensen-huang-investigation-china-spy.html), November 8, 2026.
- **Share prices:** prices at publication were saved with `yfinance`; live prices come from a small server function that
  queries Yahoo Finance when the page loads.
- **Code:** plain Python scripts (`collect.py`, `analyze.py`, `snapshot_stocks.py`).
